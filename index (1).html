How it works

Three steps, a few minutes

  1. Enter the project

    Panel and breaker sizes, solar module and inverter ratings, battery size and location, or generator details. Type them in or upload a file.

    Ohmsight runs the code checks and lists the likely problems, most serious first, with the math shown and the fix spelled out.

Questions

Frequently asked

Does Ohmsight approve my plans?

No. Ohmsight flags likely problems before you submit, and helps reviewers work faster. The building department makes the final decision on every permit.

Which code does it check against?

The checks follow the 2023 National Electrical Code, plus residential battery limits from the International Residential Code and NFPA 855. Local amendments are added for pilot jurisdictions.

What kinds of projects does it cover?

Rooftop and ground-mounted solar, home and commercial battery storage, emergency generators, legally required standby systems and optional standby generators.

Do I need to upload my drawings?

Not today. You enter the key values from the plans, or upload a simple data file. Reading values directly from spec sheets is on the roadmap.

What does it cost?

The pre-check is free while in early access. Pilot terms for building departments and pricing for the team plan are set with each partner. Use the form below to start the conversation.
